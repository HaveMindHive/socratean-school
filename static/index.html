Socratean

A tutor that teaches, then asks. Understanding you earn, in a voice that is yours.

Easier than faking it. Talk to it like a friend. Guess. Change your mind. Say what confuses you. That is the whole job, and it is what earns your certificate. Making it up takes more work than learning it.

i.

Establish your voice Four short answers, just how you would say it. There are no right answers.

ii.

Learn it, do not recite it We teach you a little, then ask a question that makes you use it. You level up by thinking in your own words, not by repeating ours.

iii.

Earn your certificate Write the paper. Its authenticity is verified against your own voice.

iv.

Nothing is hidden Your teacher can see how you write: your typing, any pasting, and time away from this page. That is how your own thinking shows.
